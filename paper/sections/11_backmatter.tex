\bmhead{Data and Code Availability}

The full source is publicly available at
\url{https://github.com/itsShrizon/Oral-Cancer},
comprising the training scripts (\texttt{train.py},
\texttt{custom\_efficientnet\_colab.py}), the evaluation script
(\texttt{evaluate\_final.py}), the explainability script
(\texttt{explain\_model.py}), the computational-metrics pipeline
(\texttt{compute\_model\_metrics.py}), and the ablation runner
(\texttt{run\_ablation.py}), together with the per-model
\texttt{classification\_metrics.json},
\texttt{performance\_metrics.json}, \texttt{training\_time.json},
\texttt{evaluation\_results.txt}, \texttt{confusion\_matrices.png}, and
\texttt{explain\_binary.png} / \texttt{explain\_subtype.png} artifacts.
Dataset~1 (the \emph{Oral Images Dataset} of Chandrashekar~\textit{et~al.},
Mendeley Data, DOI~\texttt{10.17632/mhjyrn35p4.2}, \textsc{cc by}~4.0)
\cite{chandrashekar2021oral} and Dataset~2 (the \emph{Mouth and Oral
Diseases} dataset of Rashid~\textit{et~al.}, distributed via Kaggle)
\cite{rashid2022mod,ref19} are pre-existing curated public collections;
redistribution of the original imagery is subject to the upstream dataset
licenses. The model weights for the proposed v2 model
(\texttt{results/\allowbreak custom\_\allowbreak efficientnet\_\allowbreak
v2\_\allowbreak hub\_\allowbreak v2/\allowbreak best\_\allowbreak model.pth})
and for every baseline and ablation cell are released for non-clinical
research use under the terms specified in the repository.
